CAUTION - CHANGEABLE AREA
The Kuskokwim Bay and Kuskokwim River are known to undergo constant change from year to year. Extreme caution and continuous soundings are necessary to navigate these waters. The deep draft channel in the Kuskokwim River from Kuskokwim Bay Buoy 12 northward to Bethel is marked by a series of red or green foam buoys from June 1st through October 1st each year.
