PRECAUTIONARY AREA
Traffic within the Precautionary Area may consist of vessels operating between New York Harbor and one of the established traffic lanes. Mariners are advised to exercise extreme care when navigating within these areas. The normal pilot cruising area located within the precautionary area for New York Harbor is not shown. See ENC cells US4NJ1FF, US4NJ1FG, US4NY1AQ, and US4NY1AR.
