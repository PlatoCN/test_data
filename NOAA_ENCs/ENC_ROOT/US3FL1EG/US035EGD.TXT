CAUTION - MISSILE DEBRIS AREA
Trawlers or other vessels should exercise caution while dragging the ocean floor within a 34.7 nautical miles / 40 miles radius of Cape Canaveral, Florida, since it is known that missile debris, some of which may contain unexploded ordnance, exists in this area.


WEATHER ROCKET IMPACT AREA
Mariners are cautioned against possible hazards in the impact area due to falling rocket casings.
