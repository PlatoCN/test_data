CAUTION - QUALITY OF BATHYMETRIC DATA
The Quality of data (M_QUAL) area(s) in this cell and their Category of Zone of Confidence (CATZOC) levels are estimated. The CATZOC attribute of M_QUAL is considered to have uniform quality across the data area. Mariners are cautioned that all M_QUAL areas and CATZOC levels are generalized and caution is advised when navigating near the limits of these areas, particularly for nearshore navigation or voyage planning. 

POSITIONS:
This cell has been positioned on the WGS-84 Datum through the use of a Rectified Ortho-photo.

IMAGERY: 
Commercial imagery (JUL 2023, FEB 2024, JAN 2025 FEB 2025) was used to compile certain features within this cell. 

CAUTION 1:
Use this chart with caution as considerable changes in many places have been reported (1942).

CAUTION 2:
Lights in Haiti have been reported extinguished or unreliable and buoys are frequently out of position.

CAUTION 3 - OIL AND GAS PIPELINES
Mariners are advised not to anchor or trawl in the vicinity of pipelines. Gas from a damaged pipeline could cause an explosion, loss of a vessel's buoyancy or some other serious hazard. Pipelines are not always buried and their presence may effectively reduce the charted depth.
