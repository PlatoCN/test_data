CAUTION - UNEXPLODED ORDNANCE 
Mariners are cautioned against anchoring, dredging, or trawling in this area due to the possible existence of unexploded ordnance. If unexploded ordnance is encountered, retreat and immediately make all reports to the United States (US) Coast Guard Arctic Command Center via phone at 1-866-396-1361. For additional information about unexploded ordnance, visit the United States (US) Army Technical Center for Explosives Safety at https://www.denix.osd.mil/uxo/.
